- `kb.py index` and `check` passed for all 215 entries, including provenance hashes, links and CSV equality.
- Retrieval checks passed for white-screen and plugin symptoms, case-insensitive Tauri filtering, common inclusion, adapter-only exclusion, runtime-label rejection and default database resolution from another cwd.
- All new/extended knowledge has `evidence_tier=source-reviewed` and `pc_validation=not-run`. Proposed checks and the config-overlay workaround were not executed against an application.
- No source-project setup, migration, build, signing, install, simulator, emulator or physical-device workflow ran. Clones were read-only research checkouts outside the database. Existing dirty workspace content and imported maps/snapshots were preserved.
